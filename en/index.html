What this platform is

Afula Real Estate Market (שוק הנדל״ן של עפולה והסביבה) is a regional platform, not a national classifieds board. It covers one area in depth: Afula, Afula Illit, Migdal HaEmek and the moshavim, kibbutzim and communities of the Jezreel Valley.

Every listing comes from a licensed real estate agency that has signed the platform's code of ethics — ten binding clauses covering licensing, full disclosure, a ban on bait listings, fees agreed in writing in advance, and open cooperation between agencies. Agents who breach it lose the quality badge on their profile and on every listing they publish.

Using the site as a buyer or renter is free, and no account is required.

Buying in Afula: what to know

  • Rooms, not bedrooms. A "4-room" apartment usually means three bedrooms plus a living room.
  • The safe room. Most apartments built since 1993 include a ממ״ד — a reinforced concrete room required by law. Older buildings rely on a shared shelter instead.
  • Agent's fee. In Israel the buyer normally pays the agency a fee, commonly around 2% plus VAT, agreed in writing before viewings begin. The code of ethics requires that agreement up front.
  • Purchase tax. Rates differ sharply between a first home, an additional home and a non-resident buyer. Check the current brackets with a lawyer or tax adviser before you commit.
  • Mortgages. Israeli banks lend up to 75% of the value for a first home and less for an additional or investment property. Non-residents face a lower ceiling. The site's mortgage calculator is indicative only.
  • Always use a real estate lawyer. Conveyancing in Israel is done by lawyers, not notaries, and the contract is where the real protection sits.

This is general orientation, not legal, tax or financial advice. Rates and rules change; verify everything with a licensed professional before signing.

Working with an agency

Each listing shows the agency and the agent handling it, with a direct contact button. Enquiries about a specific property reach that agent. Many agents in the area work in English and Russian as well as Hebrew — the agencies page lists every office with its areas and specialities.

Looking for a mortgage adviser, a property appraiser, a real estate lawyer, an architect or an interior designer? The professionals section on the home page lists them with direct contact details.

For agencies and professionals

Real estate agencies join through the agency sign-up page: a dedicated office page, a CRM for listings, agents and leads, and the quality badge once the code of ethics is accepted.

Property professionals — mortgage advisers, appraisers, lawyers, architects, interior designers — can publish a profile card for ₪350 per month, cancellable at any time, through the professional sign-up page. Both sign-up flows are in Hebrew.
